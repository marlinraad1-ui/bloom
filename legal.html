Terms of use

These terms apply to the Bloom app and website (“Bloom”), offered by the person named in the Impressum (“we”). By using Bloom you agree to them.

1. What Bloom is

Bloom helps you track fasting, water, steps, weight, food and workouts, gives motivation, a chat buddy called Mochi, and an optional community. Most features are free. Bloom VIP is an optional paid subscription.

2. Not medical advice

Bloom is for general wellness and motivation only. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any disease and is not a substitute for a doctor. Fasting is not safe for everyone. Do not use the fasting features if you are under 18, pregnant or breastfeeding, have diabetes, heart, kidney or liver disease, take medication, or have or had an eating disorder, unless your doctor agrees. Fasts longer than 72 hours should only be done with medical supervision. Stop and get medical help if you feel faint, dizzy, confused, have chest pain or feel very unwell. In an emergency call your local emergency number (112 in Europe).

Long fasts are at your own risk. Bloom lets adults set fasts of up to 15 days. Before any fast longer than 3 days you must confirm that you understand the risks (including fainting, electrolyte problems and refeeding syndrome), that a doctor should check you first, and that you fast on your own responsibility.

Evil Mochi

Evil Mochi is an optional, adults-only joke character that is toxic on purpose. It insults you and makes fat jokes and comments about your own body and weight, which can feel like fat shaming. You only get it after you agree to this. If you don't agree, or jokes about your body could hurt you, use Sweet Mochi. Its safety rules still apply: if you mention feeling unwell, self-harm or an eating disorder it stops joking.

Calories, body fat and other numbers are estimates from standard formulas or AI and can be wrong.

3. Who can use Bloom

Privacy policy

Who is responsible

The person named in the Impressum is responsible for your data (controller under the GDPR).

Data that stays on your phone

Your fasting history, weight log, water, steps, food log, plans, body measurements, progress photos and vision board are saved only on your device. We never receive them unless you choose to post them or send them to Mochi. Steps from Health Connect or Apple Health are read on your device and stay there. You can delete all of it in Settings.

Data we process on our servers

  • Account: email address and password (stored encrypted), so you can log in. Without an account Bloom creates an anonymous guest ID.
  • Community: your name, emoji or photo, bio, banner, posts, comments, likes, friends and chat messages, and the stats you choose to share. Your profile and posts are visible to other Bloom users.
  • Mochi chat and AI food calculator: the messages and food photos you send, plus a short summary of your current fast and goals so the reply is personal. They are sent to Google (Gemini API) to create the reply and are not stored by us.
  • Reports: what you report and why, kept up to 12 months.
  • VIP: whether you are VIP and until when, and your Stripe customer ID. Card details are handled only by Stripe; we never see them. Payment records are kept as tax law requires (up to 10 years).

Why (legal basis)

To provide the app and your account (contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Health related information you choose to share with Mochi or the community is processed only because you send it (your consent, Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR, which you can withdraw by deleting it). Safety and moderation (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f)). Tax records (legal obligation, Art. 6(1)(c)).

Service providers

  • Supabase (database, login, file storage, server functions).
  • Google (Gemini AI; Google Fonts for the app’s fonts).
  • Stripe (payments).
  • GitHub Pages (hosting the website), jsDelivr and cdnjs (app files and icons), Pexels and YouTube (workout videos), LottieFiles animations stored in the app.

Some of these providers are in the USA. Transfers are protected by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

Delete your account

In the app: open ⚙️ Settings → Delete my account → confirm. This immediately deletes your account, profile, posts, comments, likes, friends, chats, uploaded photos and VIP billing link, and cancels an active Stripe VIP subscription.

Without the app: email us from the address you signed up with (see Impressum) with the subject “Delete my Bloom account”. We delete it within 30 days. Payment records required by tax law are kept as described above.

Data saved only on your phone is removed when you uninstall Bloom or tap Reset all in Settings.
