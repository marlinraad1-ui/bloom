# Bloom 🌸

An all-in-one fasting buddy that runs on phones and PCs (installable web app).

- Water and dirty fast tracker with an hour-by-hour body-stage update
- Day plans with a checklist, alarms, notifications and voice input
- BMI, weight log and a calorie breakdown (sleep, steps, workouts, fasting)
- Workouts with calories for your weight, plus a step counter
- A gentle chat buddy that tells you to stop the fast and get help when needed
- Three styles: Kawaii, Dark Classic and Simple

All data stays on your own device. Bloom is not medical advice.
