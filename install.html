🤖 Android step sync + alarms

⬇️ Download Bloom for Android
  1. Tap the button and download Bloom.apk (6 MB).
  2. Open it. Android asks to allow installing from this source. Tap Settings, turn it on, go back.
  3. Tap Install. If Play Protect warns you, tap Install anyway. Bloom isn't in the Play Store yet, so Android doesn't know it.
  4. Open Bloom and connect Health Connect on the Move tab for all-day steps.

🍎 iPhone

  1. Open this site in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (the square with the arrow).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

It works like an app. Type your steps from Apple Health on the Move tab.

💻 Computer

Open Bloom in your browser

In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon in the address bar to add it like an app.
